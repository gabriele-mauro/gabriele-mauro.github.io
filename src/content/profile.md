I'm a third-year Computer Engineering student at Politecnico di Torino. I work on graph algorithms, mostly dynamic ones: the graph changes over time and the algorithm has to keep its answer up to date without recomputing it from scratch. I like implementing algorithms that have only been analysed on paper and seeing how they actually perform.

From March to October 2026 I was a visiting researcher in Gramoz Goranci's group at the University of Vienna. There I built, as far as we know, the first implementation of the fully dynamic spectral sparsifier of Abraham et al. (FOCS 2016), and then worked with the group on a new one based on low-diameter decompositions.
